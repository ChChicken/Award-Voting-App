How to vote

  1. A

    Open this page in Safari or Chrome, not inside Facebook, Instagram or a text message. Tip: save it to your home screen so it's one tap away tomorrow.

  2. B

    Tap Vote next to a business. The FourLeaf ballot opens in a new tab, already on that business.

  3. C

    On the FourLeaf page, scroll down past the orange 2027 Voting Is Now Open! banner to the business's name and tap VOTE. The button changes to ✓ VOTED.

  4. D

    First time only: FourLeaf asks you to sign in or create a free account. After that it remembers you, as long as you keep using the same browser.

  5. E

    Come back to this tab and tap the next one. We check off each business as you go, and the checkmarks clear at midnight so you can vote again tomorrow.

⭐ Short on time? Tap the star next to the places you love, then choose My picks to vote for just those each day.
  • One vote per person, per category, per day, from October 1 through December 15.
  • Five categories have two Lynbrook nominees. You get one vote in a category each day, so choose one. Once you vote for one, the other locks until tomorrow. You can switch which one you support on a different day.
  • Use only your own FourLeaf account. FourLeaf checks for fraud and removes suspicious votes, so please don't make extra accounts.
  • If FourLeaf asks you to sign in again, you've probably opened it in a different browser or app. Switch back to Safari or Chrome.
  • Share this page with friends and family. Every person voting daily makes a difference.
