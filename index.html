How to vote and rules
  1. A

    Open this page in Safari or Chrome, not inside Facebook, Instagram or a text message. Tip: save it to your home screen so it's one tap away tomorrow.

  2. B

    Tap Vote next to a business. The FourLeaf ballot opens, already on that business. On a computer it opens in a second tab, and every vote reuses that same tab.

  3. C

    On the FourLeaf page, scroll down past the orange 2027 Voting Is Now Open! banner to the business's name and tap VOTE. The button changes to ✓ VOTED. If you see CHANGE VOTE instead, you already voted in that category today, so leave it alone.

  4. D

    First time only: FourLeaf asks you to sign in or create a free account. After that it remembers you, as long as you keep using the same browser.

  5. E

    Come back here. On a phone, use Back: swipe in from the left edge on an iPhone, or tap Back on Android. On a computer, click this page's tab. Then tap the next one. We check off each business as you go, and the checkmarks clear at midnight so you can vote again tomorrow.

Only want to vote for a few? Tap + MY LIST under each business you want. It turns green and is saved to your list. Then tap MY LIST above the search bar to show just those businesses. The page stays on your list until you tap All. Your list stays saved on this phone or computer.

Rules and tips

  • One vote per person, per category, per day, from October 1 through December 15.
  • One category, Financial Advisor, has two Lynbrook nominees. You get one vote there each day, so choose one. If you tap the second one on FourLeaf, it moves your vote instead of adding one. Once you vote for one here, the other locks until tomorrow.
  • If FourLeaf shows CHANGE VOTE instead of VOTE, you've already voted in that category today. Don't tap it, or your vote moves to that business.
  • Use only your own FourLeaf account. FourLeaf checks for fraud and removes suspicious votes, so please don't make extra accounts.
  • If FourLeaf asks you to sign in again, you've probably opened it in a different browser or app. Switch back to Safari or Chrome.
  • Share this page with friends and family. Every person voting daily makes a difference.
